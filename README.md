StrideSense is a 12–15 gram clip-on pod that attaches to any shoe and delivers real-time 
biomechanical analysis, gait health scoring, fatigue detection, injury risk alerts, and AI coaching - all processed on-device at the edge. 
It serves two audiences simultaneously: 
Athletes & Sport Enthusiasts: real-time performance metrics with sport-specific modes 
(running, cricket, football, basketball, badminton, tennis), fatigue alerts via haptic buzz, and 
injury prevention coaching. No phone needed for alerts - the pod buzzes on your foot the 
moment your form degrades. 
General Health & Wellness: continuous gait health scoring for everyday users. Clip it to your 
regular shoes and walk normally. The pod detects gait abnormalities that may indicate 
orthopaedic issues: excessive pronation (knee/ankle problems), asymmetric gait (hip issues), 
shuffling gait (neurological concerns), and impact loading changes (bone stress risk). This is 
NOT a medical device - it's a wellness product that flags when you should see a doctor.

same - one SKU, new sports unlocked via software updates. 
2. Cricket Mode: The Category Definer: 
No competitor in the world offers cricket-specific biomechanics. This alone is a category
defining feature for India, UK, Australia, and the Caribbean. 
What it tracks: 
• Bowling run-up speed and consistency analysis 
• Delivery stride length measurement 
• Front foot landing impact force (injury risk for fast bowlers) 
• Bowling workload tracking (cumulative deliveries + impact loading per session — critical 
for fast bowler injury management) 
• Batting stance weight transfer analysis 
• Running between wickets: acceleration, deceleration, turn efficiency 
3. Gait Health Features (For Everyone): 
These work in everyday mode - clip the pod to regular shoes and walk or run normally. 
Gait Health Score (0 to 100): Composite score based on cadence regularity, stride symmetry, 
impact loading, pronation angle, and ground contact time consistency. Tracked daily over 
weeks and months to detect gradual changes. 
Pronation Alert: Excessive pronation (>8°) flagged as a risk factor for knee pain, shin splints, 
and plantar fasciitis. Suggests shoe type (stability vs neutral). 
Asymmetry Detection (dual pod setup): Left vs right differences in ground contact time, 
loading rate, and push-off power. Asymmetry >8% flagged for attention - common early 
indicator of hip, knee, or ankle issues. 
Impact Loading Trend: Tracks impact loading rate over weeks. A sudden increase is the 
number one predictor of tibial stress fractures. Alerts the user to reduce training load. 
Shuffling Gait Detection: Reduced stride length and ground clearance trending downward 
over time can indicate early neurological changes. The pod flags this as a general wellness 
observation, not a diagnosis.
